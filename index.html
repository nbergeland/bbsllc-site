03 — How We Work

A small team, shipping.

We avoid the consulting tropes — no army of juniors, no inflated decks, no boilerplate. Every engagement is led by a principal who writes code.

i

Frame

Tight scoping. We define the decision the work needs to support, the data we’ll need, and the bar for “done.”

ii

Build

Production-quality code from day one. Type-checked, tested, version-controlled. No throwaway notebooks masquerading as deliverables.

iii

Validate

Parity testing against legacy systems where they exist. Cross-validation against ground truth where it doesn’t. Numbers we can defend.

iv

Hand off

Documentation written for the engineer who inherits this in three years. Repos, schemas, environment specs — all of it.
